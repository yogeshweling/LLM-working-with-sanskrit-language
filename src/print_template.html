श्रीललितासहस्रनामस्तोत्रम्

Lalita Sahasranama — letter marking

ख घ भ ष हMahaprana क्ष स्तSamyuktakshara दीक्षाDirgha Samyukta = long letter just before a conjunct

Mahaprana: the aspirates ख घ छ झ ठ ढ थ ध फ भ and the two ushma letters ष and ह (श and स are not marked). A conjunct containing one of these is shown pink as a whole. Samyuktakshara: every conjunct, including reph forms. Dirgha Samyukta: the long-vowel letter (आ ई ऊ ॠ ए ऐ ओ औ, whether standing alone or as a vowel sign on a consonant) that comes immediately before a conjunct; the conjunct itself is only yellow. The reading is continuous, so a long letter at the end of a word or before a hyphen counts if the next word begins with a conjunct. Totals: Mahaprana {{MP}}, Samyuktakshara {{SY}}, Dirgha Samyukta {{DG}}.
